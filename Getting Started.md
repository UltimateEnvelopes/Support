    - **Categories Sheet**
        
        The Ultimate Envelopes sheet requires the Categories Sheet from the Tiller Foundations Template. However, I have made a few adjustments to the stock Tiller Category Sheet to add additional functionality.
        
        ![As you can see, I have added the columns in Brown/Orange (Columns E-N above) to the stock Tiller Category Sheet.](Getting%20Started/Untitled.png)
        
        As you can see, I have added the columns in Brown/Orange (Columns E-N above) to the stock Tiller Category Sheet.
        
        Here is a description of each Field
        
        - **Funding Account:** This is the bank account where the cash for this envelope actually is.
            
            <aside>
            💡 For example: Money for Groceries is in your Checking Account, while money for your Annual Car Insurance is in a savings account.
            
            </aside>
            
        
        - **Hide from Funding:** Used to hide categories that you don’t want to see on the Envelope Tab but may want in other sheets.
            
            <aside>
            💡 For example, I hide all Income and Investment Categories as I don’t track them as envelopes.
            
            </aside>
            
        
        - **Favorite:** Select the few Envelopes that you access all the time.
            
            <aside>
            💡  You can then view them in the View By Favorite dropdown
            
            </aside>
            
            ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%201.png)
            
        
        - **Sweep**: If selected this will enable the Category (Envelope) to be swept.
            
            <aside>
            💡 Sweeping means to zero out the envelope and make the money available to fund other envelopes. Please note that this doesn’t actually move any money in the real world, it just changes the envelope balance in the sheet.
            
            </aside>
            
        
         
        
        - **Savings Target:** You can now set a savings target and track your progress on the Envelope Sheet.
            
            <aside>
            💡 For Example: If you want to save 2000 for your car insurance payment.  Enter $2000 in this field.  You can easily see if an Envelope has a Savings Target by Looking for the Money Bag symbol on the Envelopes Tab
            
            </aside>
            
            ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%202.png)
            
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%203.png)
        
        - **Template Columns** (4 by default) – These are the Funding Template locations that you can use to speed up the filling of Envelopes.
            
            <aside>
            💡
            
            You can name these to anything you want (Paycheck 1, PayCheck 2, Monthly Total…)
            
            </aside>
            
        - **Renewal Date:**  This is a manual field that I use to track when annual payments are due
            
            <aside>
            💡 For Example: I like to remind myself when a large payment ( like Car Insurance ) is due and helps ensure that I have enough saved to make the payment.
            
            </aside>
            
        
        - **Monthly Budget Columns:** The rest of the columns are just the monthly budget amounts.
            
            > Note: In Ultimate Envelopes the monthly budget is not how much I plan to spend, but how much money I plan to fund (or fill) the envelope with that month.
            > 
            
            <aside>
            💡 Tip:  If your sheet running out of future months, It is easy to add new columns to the right of existing ones and just drag the formula in the Header row to add new months.
            
            ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%204.png)
            
            </aside>
            
        
    - **Funding Transactions**
        
        The **Funding Transactions Sheet** is what is used to calculate the Envelopes balances.  This sheet contains a full listing of all the funding transactions broken out by Category.  This allows you to see exactly when and what has changed in your envelopes.  
        
        <aside>
        ⚠️ This is more than a paper-trail. Changes made to this sheet and its values will impact your Envelope Balances.  So be careful.
        
        </aside>
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%205.png)
        
        While most of the fields are self-explanatory **(Description, Category, and Amount)** I do want to explain the difference between **Date and Date Added**.
        
        **Date:** The Date field is also known as the **Funding Date**. Or the date that you want to the funding to be added or removed from the envelope.  For example, if you have funded all envelopes in the current month and still have money left-over, you can begin to fund next month's envelopes.  To do this change the funding date to a future date.  You can do this manually in this sheet or by using the funding templates ON the Funding Section of the Envelopes Tab (Recommended)
        
        **Date Added:**  This is the actual date that you made the funding.  This date has no impact to your Envelopes

- **🆕 Setting your correct initial funding balance**
    
    After Connecting to Tiller and downloading all new transactions and bank balances
    
    - **Categorize all Transactions.**
        
        On the Transactions Sheet go through each transaction and categorize every transaction.
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2015.png)
        
        > Transactions categorized with a Type = Income ( i.e Paycheck) will increase (+) the available to funds amount.
        
        Transactions categorized with an Type = Expense ( i.e Groceries) will decrease (-) the available to funds amount as well as reduce the specific envelope balance.
        > 
        > 
        > Transactions categorized with an Type = Transfer ( i.e  Account Transfer or Credit Card Payment) will have no impact to the Available to Fund  or Envelope Balance
        > 
        
        **A**fter all categories have been categorized your envelope balances will be incorrect ( Probably negative)**. That is ok, we will fix it in the next step**
        
    - **Ensure you’re viewing the correct budget month.**
        
        From the Envelopes sheet make sure the correct month and year are selected in the drop downs.
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2016.png)
        
    - **Reset existing Envelope balances to zero**
        - Open Funding Section on the Envelope Sheet
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2017.png)
        
        - In the Fund Amount Column enter an amount that will make the envelope balance go to $0
            
            For Example:
            
            For “Cell” enter 25.00 ( the value is positive since the envelope amount is negative)
            
            For “Internet” enter -25.22 ( the value is negative since the envelope is positive)
            
            **Do this for all of the listed envelopes.**
            
        - In the Funding Date cell (cell X4) – enter a date that is before the period you want to start budgeting in.
        
        ![For example, if you are wanting to start this budget on Jan 1 2023 – Enter 12/31/22](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2018.png)
        
        For example, if you are wanting to start this budget on Jan 1 2023 – Enter 12/31/22
        
        - From the **Select Action** drop down select - **Fund Envelopes**
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2019.png)
        
        **This will write the values into the Funding Transactions Sheet and $0 zero out the envelopes.**
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2020.png)
        
    - **Set Available funds equal to current bank balance**
        
        <aside>
        💡 You are almost done, all that is left is to enter in an adjustment transaction to make your Available to Fund Balance equal to current bank balances.
        
        Why is that?  Ultimate Envelope Calculates the available to Fund amount by looking at all Income Transactions in the transactions sheet.  However, it is very likely that there are missing income transactions that your sheet doesn’t have.  This causes a mismatch that we need to correct.
        
        </aside>
        
        In order to calculate how large of an adjustment we need to enter. We need two values.
        
        **Total Bank Balance and the Current Available to Fund Balance**
        
        **Total Bank Balance**
        
        You can find your current bank balance on the **Balances Section of the Envelope Sheet**.
        
        ![In this example, the total Bank Balance is $35,793.16 (Highlight in Red)
        ](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2021.png)
        
        In this example, the total Bank Balance is $35,793.16 (Highlight in Red)
        
        **Available to Fund Balance**
        
        This is located in the Funding section
        
        ![In this example the Available to Fund is $14,568.58 ( highlighted in Orange)](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2022.png)
        
        In this example the Available to Fund is $14,568.58 ( highlighted in Orange)
        
        Calculate the difference to get our adjustment values
        
        Bank Balance   - Available to Fund  = Adjustment Amount
        
        Or
        
        $35,793.16 - $14,568.58 = $21,224.58
        
        **Our adjustment amount is $21,224.58**
        
        - Create Manual Adjustment in the Transactions Sheet
            
            From the Transactions Sheet
            
            - Add a new Row ( Below the Header (first) Row)
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2023.png)
        
        <aside>
        🔢 **Date = Same date as before (12/31/22)**
        
        **Description = Initial Balance Adjustment**
        
        **Category = Initial Balance (or another income category)**
        
        **Amount = $21,224.58**
        
        </aside>
        
        After Entering, the Available to fund balance now matches the bank balance
        
        ![Untitled](Getting%20Started/Untitled%2024.png)
