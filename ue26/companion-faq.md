## Getting the App

### Where do I get the companion app?

Open **[ultimateenvelopes.com/app](https://www.ultimateenvelopes.com/app)**. There's nothing to copy or deploy: sign in with Google and pick your budget sheet. The [Getting Started guide](/ue26/companion-setup/) walks through it.

### Do I need the UE Sheet to use the app?

No. The first time you open the app, you choose which kind of spreadsheet you're connecting to:

- **Ultimate Envelopes:** full envelope budgeting, including Envelopes, Payday funding, Balances, and Transactions.
- **Tiller Foundation:** budget vs. actuals for a Tiller Foundation Template sheet, plus Balances and Transactions.

### Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. Open your app link on your phone, then:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu and choose **Add to Home Screen**.

You'll get an icon that opens the app in one tap.

### Does the companion app require a login?

Yes, your Google account. You sign in with Google's standard sign-in and allow access once during [setup](/ue26/companion-setup/). The app can open only the budget sheet you picked, nothing else in your Drive.

Everyone uses the same link. Each person signs in with their own Google account and sees only their own sheet.

### Does my data leave Google?

No. The app reads and writes your Google Sheet directly, and your amounts and balances stay in your Google account. To show merchant logos, the app asks Google's icon service for the merchant's website icon. Only the merchant's web address is sent, never your transactions.

## Using the App

### How do I add a transaction?

On the **Transactions** tab, tap **＋** at the end of the row at the top (if you don't see the row, tap **Transactions** in the tab bar again). Fill in the description, amount, envelope, and account. It shows in the app right away and saves to your Google Sheet in the background.

### Can I customize which columns are visible?

Yes. Tap the tab again in the tab bar to open its tools panel, then **More Settings**, and check the columns you want. The order you check them is the order they appear. See [Envelope Settings](/ue26/companion-envelopes/#envelope-settings) and [Balance Settings](/ue26/companion-balances/#balance-settings).

On a phone, envelopes are shown as cards, so envelope column choices only appear on a computer.

### Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Open the same app link on any phone or computer.

Which groups you've expanded or collapsed is remembered separately for phone-sized and computer-sized screens, so each keeps a layout that fits.

### Why does it say "Showing cached data"? <span class="badge-new"><i class="fas fa-star" aria-hidden="true"></i> New</span>
{: #why-does-it-say-showing-cached-data}

To open quickly, the app first shows the data it saved on your device last time, then reads your sheet for anything newer. **Showing cached data - refreshing…** means the update is on its way; a green **Viewing recent cached data** means your data was read less than 30 minutes ago, so nothing needs reloading. Tap ↻ at the bottom left any time to reload everything fresh. See [How the App Loads Your Data](/ue26/companion-settings/#how-the-app-loads-your-data).

### Does the companion app work offline?

No. The app needs an internet connection to read from and write to your Google Sheet.
