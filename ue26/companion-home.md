Get a quick pulse on your budget the moment you open the app: account balances, envelope health (how many envelopes are overspent this month), your favorite envelopes with balance bars, and recent transaction activity, all in one view.

The **Insights** tab is the first tab in the tab bar (it used to be called Home).

## The Cards <span class="badge-updated"><i class="fas fa-pen" aria-hidden="true"></i> Updated</span>
{: #the-cards}

Tap a card to open or close it. A few cards have extra controls:

- **Top Envelopes:** your biggest envelopes for the period. Use the **Expense**, **Income**, and **Transfer** pills to choose what's included; each type has its own color. Tap the period (for example **this month**) to switch between this week, month, quarter, and year. Tap an envelope to open its details.
- **Recent Transactions:** the same type pills and colored dots, a list that matches the period in the card's header, and the date under each amount.
- **Account Balances:** each balance shows when it was last updated.
- **Envelope Health:** how many envelopes are overspent. A $0.00 balance doesn't count as overspent.

## Arrange the Dashboard <span class="badge-new"><i class="fas fa-star" aria-hidden="true"></i> New</span>
{: #customize-the-dashboard}

Tap **Edit layout** at the top right of the cards. Each card then shows its controls:

- **Move a card:** drag it to a new spot. On a phone, press and hold a moment before dragging, so scrolling doesn't move cards by accident.
- **Change its size:** tap **S**, **M**, or **L**.
- **Hide it:** tap the eye. Hidden cards stay faded while you edit, and are gone once you're done.

Tap **Done** to save. Prefer a list? **List view**, next to Done, lets you reorder, resize, and hide cards from a list instead.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/companion-insights-edit-layout.jpg" alt="Insights tab in Edit layout: each card has a drag handle, an S M L size switch and an eye button; List view and Done are at the top right" caption="Edit layout: drag cards, pick a size, or hide them" %}

Want the app to open somewhere else? Change the **Starting Tab** on the [Settings tab](/ue26/companion-settings/).
