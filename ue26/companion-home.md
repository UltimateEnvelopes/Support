Get a quick pulse on your budget the moment you open the app. The **Insights** tab is a dashboard of cards: net worth, account balances, overspent envelopes, this month's spending, funding progress, recent transactions, and more. Each card links straight to the details behind it.

## How the Cards Work

- **The number in the header** is the card's headline: green when things look good, red when something needs a look.
- **Tap a card's header** to open or close it. Open, it shows the details; closed, just the headline. The app remembers which cards you left open.
- **Tap a row** (an envelope, account, or transaction) to jump to its details on the matching tab.
- **Small cards** always show just the headline. Make a card Medium or Large to see its details (see [Arrange the Dashboard](#customize-the-dashboard)).

## The Cards <span class="badge-updated"><i class="fas fa-pen" aria-hidden="true"></i> Updated</span>
{: #the-cards}

Tap a card below to see what it shows and what you can do with it.

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Net Worth</summary>

Your assets minus your liabilities.

- A trend line shows your net worth month by month. Move along it (or touch it on a phone) to see each month's value; tap a month to show its **Total**, **Assets**, and **Liabilities** below the chart.
- **View full breakdown** opens the net worth section on the [Balances tab](/ue26/companion-balances/).

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Account Balances</summary>

How many accounts you have, and the balances of the biggest ones.

- Each account shows its balance and when it was last updated. Tap one to open its Account Insights.
- Tap the **gear** to choose which accounts appear and how many.
- The link at the bottom opens the Balances tab.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Envelope Health</summary>

How many expense envelopes are **overspent** (below $0.00) this month. A $0.00 balance doesn't count.

- Open, it lists the overspent envelopes, worst first, with the **Total overage**.
- Tap an envelope to open its details, or **View Envelopes** to see them all on the Envelopes tab.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>This Month's Spending</summary>

What you've spent so far this month.

- A chip compares it with the same point last month (for example, **12% vs last month**), green when you're spending less and red when more.
- **Expenses**, **Transfers**, and **Income** each show a count and total. Tap one to list its transactions, and tap a transaction to open it.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Funding Progress</summary>

How much of this month's budget your envelopes have been funded, as a percentage.

- A ring shows **Funded**, **Remaining**, and the **Target** (your total budget), plus how much is **Available to fund**.
- **Go to Payday** opens the [Payday tab](/ue26/companion-payday/) to fund the rest.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Needs Attention</summary>

A to-do list. The header counts the items, and **All caught up** means there's nothing to do.

- Transactions that still need an envelope.
- Envelopes that are over budget (the three biggest, then a link to the rest).
- Accounts whose balance hasn't updated recently.

Tap an item to go straight to it.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Recent Transactions</summary>

The total and number of transactions for the period in the header.

- Tap the period (**today**, **this week**, or **this month**) to switch between them. The list shows the same period, newest first.
- Use the **Expense**, **Income**, and **Transfer** pills to choose which types are included. Each type has its own color, shown as a dot on each transaction.
- Each transaction shows its envelope, amount, and date. Tap one to open it, or **View all transactions** to go to the Transactions tab.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Top Envelopes</summary>

Your five biggest envelopes for the period, with a bar for each.

- Use the **Expense**, **Income**, and **Transfer** pills to choose what's counted (Expense on its own to start). **Expense** counts money spent, **Income** money received, and **Transfer** money moved. Each type has its own color: orange for Expense, green for Income, purple for Transfer.
- Tap the period under the count (**this week**, **this month**, **this quarter**, or **this year**) to change it.
- Tap an envelope to open its details.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Uncategorized Transactions</summary>

The total and number of transactions that still need an envelope.

- Tap a transaction to open it and choose its envelope. See [Categorizing Transactions](/ue26/categorize-transactions/).
- **All caught up** means there's nothing left to categorize.

</details>

<details class="doc-expand" markdown="1">
<summary>Favorite Envelopes</summary>

Your favorite envelopes and their balances, with a bar for each.

- Tap an envelope to open its details.
- To add one, open the envelope on the Envelopes tab and mark it **Favorite** while editing it.

</details>

Your choices on these cards (pills, periods, and accounts) are remembered by the browser you set them in.

## Arrange the Dashboard <span class="badge-new"><i class="fas fa-star" aria-hidden="true"></i> New</span>
{: #customize-the-dashboard}

Put the cards you use most at the top, make them the size you want, and hide the rest.

1. Tap **Edit layout** at the top right of the cards. Each card shows a drag handle, an **S | M | L** size switch, and an eye.
2. **Move a card:** drag it to a new spot. On a phone, press and hold a moment before dragging, so scrolling doesn't move cards by accident.
3. **Change its size:** tap **S** (Small), **M** (Medium), or **L** (Large). Small cards show just the headline.
4. **Hide it:** tap the eye. Hidden cards stay faded while you edit, so you can bring them back; once you're done they're gone from the dashboard.
5. Tap **Done**.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/companion-insights-edit-layout.jpg" alt="Insights tab in Edit layout: each card has a drag handle, an S M L size switch and an eye button; List view and Done are at the top right" caption="Edit layout: drag cards, pick a size, or hide them" %}

**Prefer a list?** While editing, tap **List view** next to Done to reorder, resize, and hide the cards from a list instead.

Your layout is saved with your app, so it's the same on every device.

Want the app to open somewhere else? Change the **Starting Tab** on the [Settings tab](/ue26/companion-settings/).
