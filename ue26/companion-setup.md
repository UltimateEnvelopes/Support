How to connect the Companion App to your Ultimate Envelopes Sheet. Perform this setup from a **desktop browser** — you'll only need to do it once.

1. Open the **Apps Script editor** from your UE Sheet (**Extensions → Apps Script**).
2. In the top right, click **Deploy → New deployment**.
3. Name the deployment in the Description field, then click **Deploy**.
4. You'll be asked to authorize the script — click **Authorize access**.
5. Click **Advanced**, then click **Go to UE Companion (unsafe)**. This warning appears because the script isn't published on the Google Marketplace — Ultimate Envelopes never has access to your data, and everything stays on your own Google Drive.
6. Check **Select all**, then click **Continue**.
7. Click the **Copy** button below the URL to copy it to your clipboard.
8. Open a new browser window — on your computer or phone — and paste the URL into the address bar.
9. When the page loads, connect your Ultimate Envelopes Sheet to the app: paste the full URL or Sheet ID of your real UE Sheet (the one with your actual data) into the prompt, then click **Connect**.
10. Refresh your browser — the Ultimate Envelopes Companion App will appear with your data.
