UE is the current release of Ultimate Envelopes. It consists of two components that work together:

---

## Components

### [Google Sheet](/ue26/google-sheet/)

The core of your budget — a Google Sheets template with tabs for Envelopes, Transactions, Balances, and Payday funding. You copy it to your own Google Drive and it's yours to keep.

### [Companion Web App](/ue26/companion-app/)

A browser-based app that connects to your sheet. Use it on your phone or desktop to check balances, add transactions, and review spending without opening the spreadsheet directly.

---

## What's New in UE

- Add Transaction side panel on desktop
- Spending History chart per envelope (12-month view)
- Balances tab settings: group by type, class, or institution
- Group expand/collapse state saved per device across sessions
- Full dark mode across all tabs
- Tiller version with automated transaction import
