## How It Works

Envelope budgeting is simple: you divide your money into named "envelopes" — Groceries, Rent, Eating Out, etc. — and you spend from those envelopes. When an envelope is empty, spending stops. No guessing, no surprises.

Ultimate Envelopes brings this system into Google Sheets, paired with a fast web app for your phone or desktop.
